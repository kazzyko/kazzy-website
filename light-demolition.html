Common questions

Is debris haul-away included?

Yes. Every demolition job includes loading and hauling away all of the debris, plus a magnetic nail sweep.

How do you price demolition?

By the size of the structure and how much debris it creates. Text a photo to (414) 388-6289 for a free estimate. The final price is set on site, once we see the job in person.

What size projects do you take on?

We focus on light residential demolition: sheds, decks, fences, hot tubs, above-ground pools, trampolines and play sets. Not sure if yours fits? Send us a photo.
