Garage Cleanouts

What a garage cleanout looks like

Stop tripping over old sports equipment, spare lumber and boxes you haven't opened in years. Our crew does all of the lifting from your garage, basement or attic, so you don't have to haul anything up the stairs or out to the curb.

Point out what stays, and we load everything else into our 14-cubic-yard trailer. Usable items are set aside for donation, metal is recycled, and we sweep the floor before we leave.

Garage & Basement Cleanouts questions

Can you clear out a basement with narrow stairs?

Yes. We're experienced with tight stairways and carry everything up carefully. If something won't fit, we can often take it apart first.

Will you take old paint or chemicals?

Tell us what you have when you request a quote. Some hazardous materials like paint, solvents and chemicals can't go in our trailer, and we'll let you know what we can take.

Can I keep some things?

Of course. Walk us through what stays before we start, and we'll work around it.
