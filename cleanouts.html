Why not just rent a dumpster?

With a roll-off dumpster you do all of the lifting yourself, it sits in your driveway for days, and a dumpster on the street may need a city permit. We bring the labor and the trailer, and we're gone the same day.

  • Labor included: we carry everything out from any room or floor
  • 14-cubic-yard trailer: holds far more than a pickup truck
  • Broom sweep: we leave the space clean when we're done
  • Donate & recycle: usable items go to local charities

Common questions

How long does a cleanout take?

Most garage and basement cleanouts take a few hours. Whole-house and hoarder cleanouts can take longer, and we'll give you a time estimate along with your price.

Do I need to sort things before you come?

No. Show us anything you want to keep, and we'll handle the rest. We set aside usable items for donation and recycle metal and appliances as we load.

How is a cleanout priced?

By how much of our 14-cubic-yard trailer it fills, from $125 for a minimum load up to $700 for a full trailer. Big cleanouts may need more than one load, and we'll estimate how many ahead of time.
