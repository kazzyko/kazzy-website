Pool Table Removal

Taking a slate table apart

Slate pool tables are extremely heavy and can't be carried out in one piece. We unbolt the rails, remove the felt and lift out the slate sections, then carry each piece out carefully.

Once everything is out, we load the frame, slate and rails into our trailer and sweep up. It's a great time to clear out the rest of the basement too.

Pool Table Removal questions

Can you remove a pool table from a basement?

Yes. We take it apart into manageable pieces and carry each piece up the stairs.

Do you move pool tables to a new home?

We focus on removal and disposal. For moving a table you want to keep, a pool table specialist is the better choice.

What happens to the slate?

It's hauled away with the rest of the table and disposed of properly.
