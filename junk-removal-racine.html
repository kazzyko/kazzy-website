Racine

Local junk removal in Racine

The city of Racine has a lot of older homes, two-flats and rental units, and that shapes the work we do here: clearing apartments between tenants, carrying furniture down from upstairs units and emptying garages and basements that have filled up over the years.

Landlords and property managers can send us an address and photos and get an estimate back quickly. Homeowners get the same simple volume pricing we use everywhere.

We cover all of the city of Racine, from downtown and the lakefront neighborhoods to the west side along Highway 31, plus nearby Mount Pleasant and Caledonia.

What you get

  • The owners on every job, from your first text to the final sweep
  • Free estimates from photos
  • Pricing by trailer space: $125 minimum, $700 for a full 14-cubic-yard load
  • Usable items donated and metal recycled
  • Open 6 AM – 9 PM, 7 days a week
