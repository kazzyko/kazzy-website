How the estimate works

Each item has an average size in cubic yards, based on standard moving-industry size charts and real junk removal loads (a full-size sofa takes up about 2). We add them up and price the total against our load sizes. If it lands in between, the price lands in between too.

  • $125 minimum: 1–2 bulky items or up to 2 cu. yd.
  • $250 at 1/4 load (3.5 cu. yd.)
  • $400 at 1/2 load (7 cu. yd.)
  • $550 at 3/4 load (10.5 cu. yd.)
  • $700 for a full trailer (14 cu. yd.)

Example: a load that fills 40% of the trailer (5.6 cu. yd.) is between a quarter and a half load, so it comes out to about $340.

Real loads vary. These are average sizes, and the actual cubic yards can be bigger or smaller depending on the size of each item. Some desks are small writing desks and others are large L-shaped office desks, a king mattress takes more room than a twin, and a side-by-side fridge is bigger than an apartment one. Boxes and bags also fill gaps, while odd shapes take more room. That's why your final price is based on the space your items actually take once they're loaded.
