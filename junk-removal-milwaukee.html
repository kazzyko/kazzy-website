Built for Milwaukee homes

A lot of Milwaukee housing is older: upper and lower duplexes, bungalows with steep basement stairs, and detached garages that open onto an alley. Those are the jobs we do every week. Milwaukee County questions

Can you load from an alley?

Yes. Many Milwaukee garages open onto an alley, and we load from wherever the trailer fits best, whether that's the alley, the street or the driveway.

Can you clear out an upper unit of a duplex?

Yes. We carry everything down the stairs for you, including mattresses, dressers and appliances.
