Local junk removal in Franklin

Franklin's larger lots and newer subdivisions mean a lot of our work here happens in the backyard: an old swing set the kids outgrew, a trampoline that's seen too many winters, a shed that's starting to lean or a hot tub nobody uses anymore. We take them apart, haul everything away and leave the yard clean.

Inside, we clear out finished basements, storage rooms and garages, setting aside anything usable for donation.

We cover all of Franklin, from the neighborhoods near Ballpark Commons and Highway 100 to the subdivisions in the south and west parts of the city.

  • Free estimates from photos
