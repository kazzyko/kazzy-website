Common questions

How much does a cleanout cost?

Cleanouts are priced by how much of our 14-cubic-yard trailer the load fills: $250 for a 1/4 trailer, $400 for a 1/2 trailer, $550 for a 3/4 trailer and $700 for a full trailer. Large properties may take more than one trailer.

Do I need to be there?

Talk to us about your situation when you request a quote. We can work around your schedule.

Can you help with estates, rentals and evictions?

Yes. We do estate, rental, eviction and foreclosure cleanouts, as well as garage, basement, office and hoarder cleanouts.

How fast can you start?

Many jobs can be scheduled within days. Call or text (414) 388-6289 and we will tell you what is open.

Where does everything go?

Usable items are donated to a local charity. Metal and appliances are recycled. Only what is truly junk goes to the dump.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, card and check. A 3% fee applies to electronic payments.
